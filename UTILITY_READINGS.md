# B4 utility readings — implementation checklist

Next client feature after AI removal passes live verification. No implementation is marked complete here.

## Behavior and data

Electricity and water each have a dated room meter history. Store measured values as integer thousandths; calculate money in currency minor units. A property's tariff is the default, with an explicit room override. Store the exact tariff and reading interval on each calculated charge so later price changes cannot rewrite history.

Initial reading establishes a baseline without inventing consumption. A normal reading must be later than the previous reading and not lower. Meter replacement/reset is an explicit event with the old meter's final reading, the new meter's starting reading and a required explanation. A missed month remains one longer measured interval; do not fabricate monthly readings. Tiered tariffs use owner-entered thresholds/rates with validation and an effective date; no unverified current EVN prices are prefilled.

Invoices use server-calculated, unbilled reading intervals. A reading linked to an invoice cannot be silently edited or billed twice. Corrections must preserve the original audit record and require the associated invoice correction workflow. B6 combines these charges into period invoices.

## Situations to cover

- Owners/co-owners and scoped roles: read requires the relevant lease/financial access; recording requires lease-management authority; tariff changes additionally require price-override permission. Closed organizations, suspended/revoked members, outsiders and foreign property/room IDs are refused by the server.
- v2 writes are callable-only. Legacy v1 screens/fields remain readable and are not silently converted into meter histories or invoices.
- Empty property/room lists; no tariff, no previous reading, zero usage, missing unit/currency, loading/error/retry. Every error has a way back or retry; no fabricated success.
- Duplicate dates, earlier readings, negative/fractional/oversized inputs, zero rate, decimal VND/USD, crossed tiers, incomplete tariff bands and changing room/property currency.
- Month/year boundaries, leap days, property time zone, missed months, tenant move-in/out inside a measured interval, meter replacement/reset. Do not assign another tenant's usage without an explicit allocation decision.
- Fixed operation IDs across retries; interrupted responses, double taps, simultaneous readings, concurrent tariff edits and invoice creation. Server revision conflicts require refresh.
- Historical readings, tariff snapshots, room/property deletion, organization copy/export/purge and account deletion must retain or clean related records consistently.
- Forms, readings list, history, tariff editor and invoice linkage: English/Vietnamese, populated long names, 360px phone, landscape, desktop, 100/130/200% text. Single-line concise action labels, full headings and names, actual-font screenshot inspection and no unreachable controls.
