CanHo360 Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

CanHo360 helps property managers organize buildings, rooms, tenants, bookings, and payments. This policy explains what information the app processes and why.

Information we process

When you use CanHo360, we may process your account information, organization and property records, tenant details that you choose to enter, booking and payment records, app settings, and technical information needed to keep the service secure.

If you use AI features, we process the messages, files, images, and extracted records that you submit so we can return an answer or prepare an import for your review. Imported records are not saved until you approve them.

How we use information

We use information to provide and secure the service, synchronize your data across devices, process subscriptions, respond to support requests, prevent abuse, and improve reliability. We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

CanHo360 uses Firebase for authentication, database, hosting, and server functions; Google Gemini for AI requests; and RevenueCat and the relevant app store for subscription management. These providers process information only as needed to provide their services.

Storage and retention

Your records remain in your account until you or an authorized organization administrator deletes them, or until they are no longer needed to provide the service. AI requests are handled by the service providers described above under their applicable terms.

Security

We use authenticated access, server-side authorization, and encrypted connections. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so please avoid entering information that you are not authorized to manage.

Your choices

You can review or delete records through the app and can contact us about access or deletion requests. App-store subscription cancellation and refunds are handled under the applicable store's policies.
